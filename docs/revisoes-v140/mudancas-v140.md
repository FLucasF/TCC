## 2. Abstract

**Antes:** Coding agents based on large language models (LLMs) receive persistent guidance, in project files and ~~skills,~~ whose effect on code design is little studied. This work investigates how this guidance changes the use of design patterns by these agents. In a preregistered study, 11 Claude models built the same service at four guidance levels (220 runs). A file of principles raised from 67% to 93% the runs that applied Strategy at the most complex point of the task, with no detected change in the use of the pattern where it was not justified (overengineering) and no loss of correctness overall, although Haiku ~~4.5~~ got worse. In an exploratory extension with three of ~~these~~ models (450 runs, graded by an AI verifier), correct use increased, from the unguided level to the most guided one, in nine of ten combinations of five patterns and two tasks, except for Strategy in construction. In 90 negative controls in maintenance, requests ~~in which~~ the pattern is not justified, 88 runs kept the change small, but, in construction with the skill, ~~unnecessary~~ structure rose from 4 to 9 runs out of 75. ~~In the main study, the gain was concentrated in the models that did not succeed without guidance, the only ones with room to improve.~~

**Depois:** Coding agents based on large language models (LLMs) receive persistent guidance, in project files and **skills (instruction sets on a subject),** whose effect on code design is little studied. This work investigates how this guidance changes the use of design patterns by these agents. In a preregistered study, 11 Claude models built the same service at four guidance levels (220 runs). A file of principles raised from 67% to 93% the runs that applied Strategy at the most complex point of the task, with no detected change in the use of the pattern where it was not justified (overengineering) and no loss of correctness overall, although **the weakest model,** Haiku **4.5,** got worse. **The gain appeared in the six models that did not always succeed without guidance.** In an exploratory extension with three of **the 11** models (450 runs, graded by an AI verifier), correct use increased, from the unguided level to the most guided one, in nine of ten combinations of five patterns and two tasks, except for Strategy in construction. **Overall, it rose from 19 to 58 runs out of 75 in maintenance, which started from a lower level, and from 41 to 60 in construction. In maintenance, behavior through the API fell from 61 to 54 out of 75.** In 90 negative controls in maintenance, requests **where** the pattern is not justified, 88 runs kept the change small, but, in construction with the skill, structure **created without need** rose from 4 to 9 runs out of 75.
